# PIB Telugu press-release scope and copyright review

Reviewed 2026-08-20 for a bounded, at-most-500-page Telugu crawl.

PIB's published Copyright Policy states that material may be reproduced without
prior approval when the source is acknowledged, while excluding material whose
copyright belongs to a third party. The configured source uses the official
Telugu press-release RSS feed, preserves every release URL/identifier/date/ministry
where available, requires a two-second delay and robots permission, and extracts
only the configured PIB article body. Navigation, images, social embeds, and
explicit third-party/external-content containers are excluded.

Policy URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/content/3604_2_CopyrightPolicy.aspx?lang=1&reg=3
